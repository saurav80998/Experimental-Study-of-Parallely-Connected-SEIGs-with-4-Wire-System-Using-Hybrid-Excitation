This project presents an experimental study of parallelly connected Self-Excited Induction Generators (SEIGs) with a four-wire system for rural applications.  
A hybrid excitation topology combining star- and delta-connected capacitors was developed and experimentally evaluated.  
Different machine, capacitor, and load configurations were investigated to identify suitable operating conditions.  
Parallel operation of 2.2 kW and 5.5 kW SEIGs was studied under resistive, inductive, and nonlinear loads.  
System performance was evaluated in terms of voltage regulation, load sharing, efficiency, frequency, and power quality.
